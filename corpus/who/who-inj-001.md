# Pocket Guide to Safe Injection Practices

## One Needle, One Syringe, One Time

Use a new sterile syringe and a new sterile needle for every injection and for every entry into a vial. Never reuse a syringe on another patient, even if the needle is changed, and never re-enter a vial with a syringe or needle that has been used on a patient.

## Preparing Injections

Prepare injections in a clean, designated area, away from used equipment, blood samples and waste. Clean your hands before preparing medications. Wipe the rubber septum of the vial with 70% alcohol and let it dry before piercing it. Never leave a needle in the septum for repeated withdrawals.

## Single-Dose and Multi-Dose Vials

Prefer single-dose vials. Use a single-dose vial for one patient and one injection only, and discard what is left; never pool leftover contents. Keep multi-dose vials out of the patient treatment area where possible, write the opening date on the label, and discard the vial within 28 days of opening unless the manufacturer states a different period.

## Disposal and Reporting

Put used needles and syringes into a sharps container immediately, at the point of use, without recapping. Report any reuse of syringes or other unsafe practice to the infection prevention and control team so that exposed patients can be identified and offered testing.
