# Hand Hygiene Policy for Clinical Staff

## Policy Statement

All staff working in patient-care and clinical research areas of the hospital must perform hand hygiene in accordance with this policy. Compliance with hand hygiene is a condition of clinical privileges and of access to research units, and non-compliance is addressed through the supervisory chain.

## Approved Products and Method

Alcohol-based handrub containing 60–95% alcohol is the approved method for routine hand antisepsis. Apply the product and rub all surfaces of the hands for at least 15 seconds until dry. Wash with soap and water when hands are visibly soiled and after caring for patients with diarrhoeal illness.

## Required Opportunities

Hand hygiene is required on entry to and exit from every patient room, before and after patient contact, before aseptic procedures, after contact with body fluids, and after removing gloves.

## Monitoring

Unit managers perform monthly compliance audits using a standard form, and results are reported to the hospital epidemiology service each quarter.
