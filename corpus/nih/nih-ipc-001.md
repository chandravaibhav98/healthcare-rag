# Hospital Epidemiology Service Charter

## Mission

The hospital epidemiology service runs the infection prevention and control (IPC) program for all patient-care and clinical research areas. It reports to the chief medical officer and presents an annual plan, budget and results to the infection control committee.

## Staffing

The service employs at least one full-time infection preventionist for every 250 beds, with additional staff for intensive care, transplant and other high-risk units, and has access to a hospital epidemiologist at all times.

## Education

Every staff member completes IPC training at orientation and an annual refresher, including practical assessment of hand hygiene and PPE use. Unit IPC link nurses receive additional training each quarter.

## Patient Placement Standards

Beds in multi-bed rooms are spaced at least 1 metre apart, and occupancy above the designed bed capacity is escalated to the bed management office.
