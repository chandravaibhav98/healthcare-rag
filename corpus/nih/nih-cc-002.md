# Ward Vaccine Refrigerator Procedure

## Approved Units

Wards store vaccines only in pharmacy-approved, purpose-built vaccine refrigerators that are connected to the central temperature monitoring system. Household or bar-style refrigerators, and refrigerators used for food or specimens, may not be used for vaccines.

## Required Temperatures

Refrigerated vaccines are kept at 2–8 °C. Freezers that hold vaccines are kept between −50 °C and −15 °C. The central system records the temperature every 5 minutes and alarms when a unit leaves its range.

## Daily Review

At the start of each workday, the ward vaccine coordinator reviews the temperature history and the minimum and maximum values for the previous day and documents the review in the monitoring system. The pharmacy retains the electronic temperature records.

## Responding to an Alarm

A staff member checks the refrigerator within 15 minutes of an alarm, confirms the reading and looks for a cause such as an open door or a power failure. If the temperature cannot be corrected promptly, vaccines are moved to a backup unit in qualified transport containers.

## After an Excursion

Label the affected vaccines "do not use", keep them at 2–8 °C, and notify the vaccine coordinator and the pharmacy manager immediately. Do not use or discard the vaccines until pharmacy issues a written viability determination after contacting the manufacturer and the immunization program. Every excursion is logged in the pharmacy quality database.
