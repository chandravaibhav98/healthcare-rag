# Needlestick Prevention and Safety-Engineered Devices

## Device Selection

The hospital uses safety-engineered sharps devices for all procedures for which such devices are commercially available, including safety syringes, retractable lancets, needleless intravenous connectors, blunt suture needles for fascia closure and safety scalpels. Frontline staff who use the devices take part in evaluating new products, and the evaluation is documented each year in the exposure control plan.

## Safe Handling in Patient Care and Research

Activate the safety mechanism immediately after use and before moving away from the patient or work area. Do not recap, bend or break needles. Use a neutral zone to pass sharps during surgery. In research laboratories, replace needles and glass with plastic alternatives wherever possible, and never leave sharps on benches or in biosafety cabinets after work is completed.

## Sharps Containers

Sharps containers are mounted at the point of use, within arm's reach and at a height where the opening is visible. Containers are closed and replaced when the contents reach the fill line marked at three-quarters capacity. Staff must not reach into containers or shake them to make more room. Full containers are locked and placed in the regulated waste pickup area.

## Reporting an Injury

Any needlestick, cut or splash to the eyes, nose, mouth or non-intact skin must be reported immediately to the supervisor and to occupational medicine, which is available 24 hours a day through the emergency page. After first aid, the injured worker is evaluated without delay so that post-exposure prophylaxis, when indicated, can start within hours. An online injury report is completed before the end of the shift.

## Learning From Injuries

Occupational medicine reviews every sharps injury to determine the device involved, the activity and whether a safety feature was used or could have prevented the injury. Aggregate injury data are presented to the safety committee quarterly and used to select devices and target training.
