# Hand Hygiene Quick Card for Clinical Units

## When

Clean your hands on entry to and exit from every patient room, before touching a patient, before any clean or aseptic procedure, after any body fluid exposure risk, after touching a patient, after touching the patient's surroundings, and immediately after removing gloves. Wearing gloves never replaces hand hygiene.

## Which Method

Use the hospital alcohol-based handrub, which contains 60–80% alcohol, for routine hand antisepsis. Wash with soap and water when hands are visibly soiled, after using the toilet, and when caring for patients on enteric precautions, such as patients with Clostridioides difficile or norovirus.

## How Long

Rub handrub over palms, backs of hands, between fingers, thumbs and fingertips for 20–30 seconds until dry. When washing with soap and water, wash for 40–60 seconds, then rinse and dry with a disposable towel.

## Monitoring

Independent trained observers measure compliance continuously and units see a weekly dashboard. A unit that stays below 90% compliance for two consecutive months submits an improvement plan to the hospital epidemiology service.
