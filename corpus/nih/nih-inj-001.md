# Medication Preparation and Injection Procedure

## Preparation Area

Medications are prepared in the clean medication room or a designated clean area, never at a nurses' station next to specimens or used equipment. Perform hand hygiene first. Disinfect the vial septum with an alcohol pad and allow it to dry before each entry. Needles are never left inserted in a septum.

## Syringes and Needles

A new sterile needle and syringe are used for each patient, for each injection and for each entry into a vial. Syringes are never reused for another patient, even with a new needle. Infusion bags and tubing are single-patient items and are never used as a shared source of flush solution.

## Vials

Single-dose vials are used for one patient for one procedure and the remainder is discarded; leftovers are never combined. Multi-dose vials are dedicated to one patient whenever possible. Once opened, a multi-dose vial is labeled with the date and discarded after 28 days, or sooner if the manufacturer requires it.

## Reporting Breaches

Any syringe reuse or other breach of safe injection practice is reported at once to the hospital epidemiology service and through the safety reporting system, so that patient notification and testing can be evaluated with public health.
