# Outbreak Communication and Line List Management

## Activation of the Outbreak Team

Hospital epidemiology activates the outbreak management team when a suspected outbreak is confirmed or when a single case of a pathogen of high concern is identified. The team includes hospital epidemiology, the medical and nursing leads of the affected units, microbiology, occupational medicine, environmental services, pharmacy and communications. The first meeting takes place within 24 hours of activation, and the team then meets at an agreed frequency until the outbreak is closed.

## The Line List

Hospital epidemiology maintains a single, controlled line list for each outbreak. Each row is one case and includes a unique case number, medical record number, age, sex, unit and room history, admission date, symptom onset date, specimen dates and results, devices and procedures, exposures of interest, case status (suspected, probable or confirmed) and outcome. Only the outbreak team can edit the line list, and every change is recorded with the date and editor.

## Internal Communication

Affected units hold a brief daily huddle to review new cases, compliance with control measures and supply issues. Staff receive written updates describing the case definition, the precautions required and whom to contact with questions. Messages are consistent across all units to avoid confusion.

## Communication With Patients and Families

Patients who are cases or who were exposed are informed by their treating team, supported by hospital epidemiology, with information about the infection, the precautions in place and any testing needed. Written information is provided in plain language and in the patient's preferred language.

## External Communication and Closure

Hospital epidemiology notifies the public health authority as required by law and coordinates with them on testing and case finding. Media enquiries are handled only by the communications office. The outbreak is closed by the outbreak management team, and a summary report with recommendations is circulated within 30 days of closure.
