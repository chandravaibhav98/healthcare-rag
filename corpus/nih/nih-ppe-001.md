# Respiratory Protection Program

## Program Scope

The respiratory protection program applies to every staff member who may be required to wear a tight-fitting respirator, such as an N95 filtering facepiece respirator or an elastomeric half-mask, during patient care, research involving airborne hazards or decontamination tasks. The program is administered by occupational medicine in collaboration with hospital epidemiology and industrial hygiene.

## Medical Evaluation

Before fit testing, each worker completes a confidential medical questionnaire reviewed by an occupational medicine clinician to confirm that they can safely wear a respirator. A follow-up medical examination is arranged when the questionnaire identifies a condition, such as significant cardiopulmonary disease or claustrophobia, that may affect respirator use. Medical clearance is repeated when the worker's health status changes.

## Fit Testing

Workers are fit tested with the exact make, model and size of respirator they will use, before first use and at least annually thereafter. Quantitative fit testing is the standard method; qualitative testing with a bitter or sweet test agent may be used for filtering facepiece respirators. A new fit test is required after a change of respirator model or a significant change in facial structure. Workers must be clean-shaven where the respirator seals to the face.

## Seal Checks and Use

Perform a user seal check every time a respirator is put on, by covering the respirator and exhaling and inhaling gently to confirm that there is no leakage around the edges. Discard filtering facepiece respirators after aerosol-generating procedures, when damaged, soiled or difficult to breathe through, and at the end of the shift unless an approved extended-use protocol is in effect during a supply shortage.

## Powered Air-Purifying Respirators

Workers who cannot pass a fit test, including those with facial hair for religious reasons, are provided with a loose-fitting powered air-purifying respirator (PAPR) and trained in battery checks, airflow testing and cleaning of the hood or helmet after use.
