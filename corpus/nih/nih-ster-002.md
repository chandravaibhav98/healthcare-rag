# Instrument Reprocessing Fundamentals for Clinical Staff

## Cleaning Comes First

Cleaning is the most important step in reprocessing. An instrument that still carries blood or tissue cannot be reliably disinfected or sterilized, so keep instruments moist after use and send them to sterile processing promptly in a closed, labeled container.

## Risk Classes

Instruments that enter sterile tissue or the bloodstream are critical and must be sterilized. Endoscopes and other semi-critical devices that contact mucous membranes require at least high-level disinfection, and sterilization where the device allows. Non-critical items such as blood pressure cuffs need cleaning and low-level disinfection. Single-use devices are never reprocessed.

## Sterile Storage

Store sterile packs in a clean, dry, enclosed area off the floor. Sterility is event-related, not time-related: a pack stays sterile until the wrapping is damaged, wet or opened. Check every pack before use.

## Sets and Loads

Sets are assembled with a count sheet and an internal chemical indicator, and heavy instrument sets must not exceed 11.3 kilograms.

## Immediate-Use Sterilization

Immediate-use steam sterilization is only for urgent situations when no other option exists. It is never used for implants or for convenience, and each use is logged and reviewed.
