# PPE Donning and Doffing Procedure for Isolation Rooms

## Choosing PPE

Select PPE from a point-of-care risk assessment. Gloves are worn for contact with blood, body fluids, mucous membranes and non-intact skin. Gowns protect skin and clothing. A mask or respirator protects the mouth and nose, and goggles or a face shield are added whenever splashes or sprays are likely.

## Putting PPE On

1. Perform hand hygiene.
2. Put on the gown and tie it at the neck and waist.
3. Put on the mask or N95 respirator and perform a user seal check.
4. Put on goggles or a face shield.
5. Put on gloves, pulling the cuffs over the gown sleeves.

## Taking PPE Off

1. Remove the gown and gloves together, rolling the gown inside out and peeling the gloves off with it.
2. Perform hand hygiene.
3. Leave the room or enter the anteroom.
4. Remove eye protection by the headband or arms.
5. Remove the mask or respirator by the straps without touching the front.
6. Perform hand hygiene.

## After Use

Discard single-use PPE as regulated waste. Clean and disinfect reusable eye protection before reuse. Report any breach of PPE, such as a torn glove during care, to the unit charge nurse.
