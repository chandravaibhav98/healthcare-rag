# Isolation Precautions Quick Reference Card

## Standard Precautions for Every Patient

Use standard precautions for all patients: hand hygiene, PPE chosen for the expected exposure, respiratory hygiene and cough etiquette, safe injection practices, sharps safety, cleaning and disinfection of the environment and equipment, and safe handling of linen and waste.

## Contact Precautions

Use a single room when available. Wear a gown and gloves for all contact with the patient or the patient's environment, putting them on at entry and removing them before leaving. Use dedicated equipment.

## Droplet Precautions

Use a single room, or keep patients at least 1 metre (3 feet) apart with the curtain drawn. Wear a mask on entry. Patients wear a mask whenever they leave the room.

## Airborne Precautions

Use an airborne infection isolation room with negative pressure, at least 12 air changes per hour and the door closed. Staff wear a fit-tested N95 or higher respirator. Use airborne precautions for tuberculosis, measles and varicella. If no isolation room is available, place the patient in a single room with the door closed and a mask on the patient, and transfer them as soon as possible.

## Transport

Move patients only when necessary, tell the receiving area in advance, and have patients on droplet or airborne precautions wear a mask.
