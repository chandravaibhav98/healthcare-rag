# Sequence for Putting On and Removing Personal Protective Equipment

## Selecting PPE

The type of PPE used will vary based on the level of precautions required, such as standard and contact, droplet or airborne infection isolation precautions. HCP select PPE based on the anticipated exposure. Gloves protect hands, gowns protect skin and clothing, masks and respirators protect the mouth and nose, and goggles or face shields protect the eyes. PPE must fit correctly, and HCP must be trained in its use before they need it in practice.

## Putting On PPE (Donning)

1. Gown: fully cover the torso from neck to knees and arms to the end of the wrists, and fasten it in the back of the neck and waist.
2. Mask or respirator: secure ties or elastic bands at the middle of the head and neck, fit the flexible band to the nose bridge, and fit it snug to the face and below the chin. Perform a user seal check for respirators.
3. Goggles or face shield: place over the face and eyes and adjust to fit.
4. Gloves: extend to cover the wrist of the isolation gown.

Keep hands away from the face, limit the surfaces touched, and change gloves when torn or heavily contaminated.

## Removing PPE (Doffing): Example 1

1. Gloves: the outside of the gloves is contaminated. Grasp the palm area of one gloved hand and peel off the first glove, hold it in the gloved hand, slide the fingers of the ungloved hand under the remaining glove at the wrist and peel it off over the first glove.
2. Goggles or face shield: remove from the back by lifting the headband or ear pieces.
3. Gown: unfasten ties, pull away from the neck and shoulders touching the inside only, turn it inside out, fold or roll it into a bundle and discard.
4. Mask or respirator: grasp the bottom ties or elastics, then the top ones, and remove without touching the front.
5. Perform hand hygiene immediately after removing all PPE.

## Removing PPE (Doffing): Example 2

As an alternative, HCP may remove the gown and gloves together. Grasp the gown in the front and pull it away from the body so that the ties break, touching the outside of the gown only with gloved hands. While removing the gown, fold or roll it inside out into a bundle and peel off the gloves at the same time, touching only the inside of the gloves and gown with bare hands. Then remove eye protection and the mask or respirator, and perform hand hygiene. If hands become contaminated at any step, immediately perform hand hygiene before continuing.
