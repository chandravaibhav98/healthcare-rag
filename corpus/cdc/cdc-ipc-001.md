# Infection Prevention Program Core Elements Checklist

## Leadership and Staffing

Facility leadership provides an infection prevention and control (IPC) program with written goals, an annual plan and a budget. Staff the program with at least one full-time trained infection preventionist for every 250 acute care beds, and more where there are intensive care units or high-acuity patients. A multidisciplinary infection control committee oversees the program.

## Training

All HCP receive IPC training when they start work and refresher training at least annually. Training includes competency checks, such as observed hand hygiene technique and PPE donning and doffing.

## Surveillance and Feedback

Track health care-associated infections, antimicrobial resistance and process measures such as hand hygiene and bundle compliance, and feed results back to frontline staff and leadership regularly.

## Environment and Capacity

Avoid overcrowding: keep bed occupancy within the facility's designed capacity and space beds at least 1 metre apart. Provide hand hygiene stations at every point of care, adequate ventilation and reliable supplies of PPE.
