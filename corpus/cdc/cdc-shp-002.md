# Sharps Injury Prevention Quick Reference

## Handling Needles

Do not recap used needles. If recapping cannot be avoided for a specific procedure, use a one-handed scoop technique or a mechanical recapping device. Never bend, break or remove needles by hand, and activate the safety feature immediately after use.

## Passing Instruments

Do not pass sharps from hand to hand. Use a neutral zone, such as a tray or basin, during procedures and surgery.

## Sharps Containers

Place puncture-resistant, leak-proof, labeled sharps containers within arm's reach of where sharps are used. Close and replace containers when they are three-quarters full, and never reach into or shake a container to make more room.

## Safer Devices

Use safety-engineered devices, such as retractable needles, needleless connectors and blunt suture needles, whenever they are available, and involve frontline staff in choosing them.

## If an Injury Happens

Wash the site with soap and water, report immediately, and get evaluated without delay, because HIV post-exposure prophylaxis works best when started within hours.
