# Recommended Vaccines for Health Care Personnel

## Overview

Because of their contact with patients or infective material, health care personnel are at risk for exposure to and possible transmission of vaccine-preventable diseases. Employers and HCP share responsibility for preventing occupationally acquired infection. The schedule below applies to HCP without evidence of immunity; documented prior vaccination or laboratory evidence of immunity replaces the corresponding doses.

## Schedule

| Vaccine | Recommendation for HCP |
| --- | --- |
| Hepatitis B | 2-dose or 3-dose series depending on product; test anti-HBs 1–2 months after the final dose |
| Influenza | 1 dose every year |
| MMR | 2 doses, at least 28 days apart, for HCP born in or after 1957 without evidence of immunity |
| Varicella | 2 doses, 4 weeks apart, without evidence of immunity |
| Tdap | 1 dose as soon as feasible if not previously received, then Td or Tdap every 10 years; Tdap during each pregnancy |
| Meningococcal | For microbiologists routinely exposed to Neisseria meningitidis isolates |

## Evidence of Immunity

Acceptable presumptive evidence of immunity to measles, mumps and rubella is written documentation of vaccination with two doses, laboratory evidence of immunity or laboratory confirmation of disease. A self-reported history of disease is not acceptable evidence for HCP. For varicella, evidence includes written documentation of two doses, laboratory evidence of immunity or a diagnosis or verification of a history of varicella or herpes zoster by a health care provider.

## Hepatitis B Non-Responders

HCP who do not develop protective antibody after a complete series receive a second complete series and are retested 1–2 months after the final dose. Those who remain negative after two complete series are considered non-responders and are tested for hepatitis B surface antigen. Non-responders must be counseled about the need for hepatitis B immune globulin after any known or likely exposure to an infectious source.

## Program Responsibilities

Facilities maintain a secure, up-to-date record of immunization status for every HCP, review it at hire and annually, and use it to make rapid exclusion decisions during exposures and outbreaks.
