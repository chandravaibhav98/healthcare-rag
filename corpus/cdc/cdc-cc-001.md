# Vaccine Storage and Handling

## Storage Units

Use stand-alone refrigerators and freezers designed specifically for vaccine storage. Dormitory-style or bar-style combined units are not allowed. Store vaccines in their original packaging with the lids closed until administration, and place them in the center of the unit, 2 to 3 inches away from walls, ceiling, floor and door. Never store vaccines in the doors or vegetable bins of the unit.

## Temperature Ranges

Refrigerated vaccines must be kept between 2 °C and 8 °C, with a target of 5 °C. Frozen vaccines must be kept between −50 °C and −15 °C. Never allow refrigerated vaccines to freeze.

## Temperature Monitoring

Each storage unit must have a calibrated digital data logger or thermometer. Staff must check and record the minimum and maximum temperatures twice each workday, at the beginning and end of the day, on a paper or electronic log. Keep temperature logs for at least 3 years.

## Temperature Excursions

If temperatures fall outside the recommended range, label the affected vaccines "do not use" and keep them in a unit at the correct temperature. Report the excursion to the immunization program within 24 hours and contact the vaccine manufacturer for guidance on whether the vaccines can be used. Do not discard vaccines unless directed to do so. Document the event, the actions taken and the final decision.

## Emergency Plan

Each facility must have a written plan for moving vaccines to an alternate storage location in an emergency such as a power outage or equipment failure, including the transport containers and conditioning materials to be used.
