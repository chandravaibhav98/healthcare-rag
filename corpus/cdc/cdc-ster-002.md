# Steam Sterilization Quick Reference for Sterile Processing

## Cycle Parameters

Steam is the preferred sterilant for heat-tolerant items. Typical holding times are at least 15 minutes at 121 °C in gravity-displacement sterilizers and at least 3 minutes at 132–135 °C in pre-vacuum sterilizers, following the validated cycle for the load. Packs must be dry before they leave the sterilizer.

## Daily and Weekly Tests

Run a Bowie-Dick type air-removal test each day the pre-vacuum sterilizer is used, before the first processed load. Monitor steam sterilizers with biological indicators at least weekly, and preferably daily, and with a biological indicator in every load that contains an implantable device.

## Implants

Quarantine loads containing implants until the biological indicator is negative. Release before the result is known only in a documented emergency.

## Positive Biological Indicator

Remove the sterilizer from service, notify the supervisor and infection prevention, recall items processed since the last negative indicator where possible, review the mechanical and chemical records, and retest before returning the sterilizer to use.

## Choosing the Process

Critical items that enter sterile tissue are sterilized. Semi-critical items that touch mucous membranes, such as endoscopes, need at least high-level disinfection. Non-critical items that touch only intact skin need cleaning and low-level disinfection.
