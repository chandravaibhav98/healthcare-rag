# Health Care Personnel Vaccination Quick Reference

## Measles, Mumps, Rubella and Varicella

HCP born in or after 1957 without evidence of immunity receive 2 doses of MMR vaccine at least 28 days apart. HCP without evidence of varicella immunity receive 2 doses of varicella vaccine 4 weeks apart. Acceptable evidence is written documentation of two doses, laboratory evidence of immunity or laboratory confirmation of disease; a self-reported history of measles is not acceptable evidence for HCP.

## Hepatitis B

Give a 2-dose or 3-dose series depending on the product. Test for anti-HBs 1–2 months after the final dose; a result of at least 10 mIU/mL shows protection. HCP who do not respond receive a second complete series and are retested 1–2 months after its last dose. Persistent non-responders are tested for hepatitis B surface antigen and counseled about hepatitis B immune globulin after any exposure.

## Influenza, Tdap and Td

All HCP receive influenza vaccine every year. HCP who decline influenza vaccination may be required by facility policy to wear a mask during influenza season. HCP receive 1 dose of Tdap if they have not received it before, then a Td or Tdap booster every 10 years.

## Records

Facilities keep a confidential, current immunization record for every HCP, review it at hire and every year, and use it to make exclusion decisions quickly during exposures and outbreaks.
