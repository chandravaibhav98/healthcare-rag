# Safe Injection Practices

## One Needle, One Syringe, Only One Time

Safe injection practices are part of standard precautions and are intended to prevent the transmission of bloodborne pathogens between patients and between patients and HCP. Use a new, sterile needle and a new, sterile syringe for each injection, and for each entry into a vial or medication container. Never reuse a syringe, even with a new needle, to administer medication to more than one patient, and never use the same syringe to access a medication vial after it has been used on a patient.

## Single-Dose and Multi-Dose Vials

Use single-dose vials whenever possible. Single-dose vials are used for one patient for a single case, procedure or injection, and leftover contents are discarded; they must never be combined or pooled. If a multi-dose vial is used, dedicate it to a single patient whenever possible. Multi-dose vials that enter the immediate patient treatment area must be dedicated to that patient. Once opened, date a multi-dose vial and discard it within 28 days unless the manufacturer specifies a different, shorter or longer, date.

## Aseptic Technique

Prepare medications in a clean area away from potentially contaminated items such as used equipment and specimens. Perform hand hygiene before preparing medications. Disinfect the rubber septum of a vial with alcohol before piercing it. Do not leave a needle inserted in a vial septum for multiple uses. Infusion bags and bottles are used for one patient only and are never used as a common source of supply for multiple patients.

## Sharps Disposal and Injury Prevention

Discard used needles and syringes immediately in a puncture-resistant sharps container at the point of use. Do not recap needles by hand, and activate safety features immediately after use.

## Reporting Unsafe Practices

Breaches of safe injection practices, such as syringe reuse, must be reported to the infection prevention team so that patient notification and testing can be evaluated with the health department.
