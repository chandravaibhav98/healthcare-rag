# Respiratory Protection Quick Reference for Health Care Personnel

## Medical Clearance

Before an HCP is fit tested, a licensed clinician reviews a confidential medical questionnaire to confirm the person can safely wear a respirator, with a follow-up examination when needed.

## Fit Testing

HCP who may need an N95 or other tight-fitting respirator are fit tested with the exact make, model and size before first use and annually thereafter, and again after a change of model or of facial features. Facial hair where the respirator seals prevents a reliable fit; HCP who cannot pass a fit test use a loose-fitting powered air-purifying respirator (PAPR).

## Seal Check Every Time

Perform a user seal check each time you put on a respirator: cover the facepiece, exhale and inhale gently, and feel for air leaking around the edges. Readjust or replace the respirator if it leaks.

## When to Discard

Discard an N95 after aerosol-generating procedures, when it is damaged, soiled or hard to breathe through, and at the end of the shift, unless an approved extended-use plan is in place during a shortage.

## Eye and Face Protection

Wear goggles or a face shield in addition to a mask or respirator whenever splashes or sprays of blood, body fluids or secretions are likely.
