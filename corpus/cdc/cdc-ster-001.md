# Sterilization Process Monitoring and Recall

## Purpose

Sterilization must be monitored routinely using a combination of mechanical, chemical and biological indicators, because no single indicator proves that a load is sterile. This document describes the monitoring program for steam and low-temperature sterilizers and the procedure to follow when a sterilization failure is detected.

## Mechanical and Chemical Indicators

Mechanical indicators include assessing cycle time, temperature and pressure by observing gauges or displays and reviewing the printout for each cycle. An internal chemical indicator is placed inside each package, and an external chemical indicator is used on the outside of each package unless the internal indicator is visible. Chemical indicators show that the package was exposed to the sterilization process but do not prove sterility. A pre-vacuum steam sterilizer is tested with a Bowie-Dick type test each day the sterilizer is used, before the first processed load.

## Biological Indicators

Biological indicators containing spores of Geobacillus stearothermophilus are used to monitor steam sterilizers at least weekly, and preferably daily. Every load containing an implantable device must be monitored with a biological indicator and the implant quarantined until the result is available. Biological indicators are also used after installation, relocation or major repair of a sterilizer, with three consecutive empty cycles required to pass before the sterilizer is returned to use.

## Responding to a Positive Biological Indicator

If a biological indicator is positive, remove the sterilizer from service, notify the supervisor and the infection prevention team, and retest the sterilizer. Recall and reprocess, to the extent possible, all items processed since the last negative biological indicator. Review mechanical and chemical indicator records to identify the cause. Patients on whom potentially non-sterile items were used are assessed according to a risk evaluation.

## Records

Keep sterilization records, including cycle printouts, indicator results and load contents, for the period required by regulation. Each package is labelled with the sterilizer number, cycle number and date to allow recall.
