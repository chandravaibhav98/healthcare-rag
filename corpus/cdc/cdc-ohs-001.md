# Work Restrictions for Health Care Personnel Quick Reference

## Gastrointestinal Illness

HCP with acute vomiting or diarrhea are excluded from work. HCP with norovirus gastroenteritis return to work 48 hours after their symptoms resolve and follow strict hand hygiene on return.

## Influenza-Like Illness

HCP with fever and respiratory symptoms are excluded from work until at least 24 hours after their fever ends without the use of fever-reducing medicines.

## Measles and Varicella Exposure

HCP without presumptive evidence of immunity who are exposed to measles or varicella are excluded from duty from the 5th day after the first exposure through the 21st day after the last exposure.

## Onboarding Evaluation

Before placement, occupational health reviews each new HCP's immunization status, performs baseline tuberculosis screening with a symptom review and an interferon-gamma release assay or tuberculin skin test, and provides medical clearance for respirator use when the job requires it.
